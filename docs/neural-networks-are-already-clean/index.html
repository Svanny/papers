About this edition

A rewrite condensing Nick Cammarata’s essay on neural networks and interpretability. It covers the four main parts, 28 substantive appendix sections, measurement tables, and bibliography.

The essay examines whether seemingly unrelated neuron behaviors reflect generalization and limitations of interpretability tools, and explores how native neurons and circuits can be studied directly.
