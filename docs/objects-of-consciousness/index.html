About this edition

A rewrite condensing Hoffman and Prakash’s paper on perception, conscious agents, and conscious realism. It retains the ten main sections, mathematical definitions, and objections and replies.

The paper develops a formal model of interacting conscious agents and explores how perceived objects and spacetime might emerge from their interactions.

The concepts and arguments belong to the original authors. This adaptation does not claim original research or authorship by this blog.
