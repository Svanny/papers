# Blog instructions

## Presentation preferences

- Do not add eyebrow labels or subtitles. Use direct titles and substantive body text.
- Clearly attribute adaptations to the original authors; do not present condensed rewrites as original research by the blog owner.
- Use the same compact archive rows for papers and condensed readings. Include original-publication links for condensed entries.
- Offer PDF as the public download for condensed readings; archive supplied source bundles outside `docs/`.
